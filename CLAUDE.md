## Project intent
A private-development, story-driven demonstrator for Tatoma: help office/desk workers recognise their work in a possible AI-supported future.
The twin is not a factual career prediction, an autonomous digital employee or a psychological assessment.
Understand the person before portraying their future. Prioritise an interesting, recognisable conversation and credible examples.
Do not narrow the audience to one profession without approval.

## Critical product decisions
- Interview: ongoing model-led conversation, not a longer fixed questionnaire.
- Retain common grounding topics but allow contextual follow-ups; ordinarily one model request updates notes and asks the next question.
- Accept brief informative answers. Conversational brevity is not an information-quality score or a workplace-writing preference.
- Honour skips/refusals, limit unproductive probing, preserve unknowns and allow natural-language stopping.
- Do NOT add visible “Ask it differently” or mid-interview “Review what you have” controls.
- Final profile-review design is undecided. The delivered grouped check is a prototype safeguard, not a settled final UX.
- Separate original answers, tentative interpretations, participant-approved statements and hypothetical future scenes.
- Keep source-linked evidence; a matching quotation does not prove an interpretation is correct.
- Corrections/retractions must not reappear through original transcripts, stale profile IDs or hidden summaries.
- Three personalised starting buttons are generated by the model, not a template bank; persist for a profile version.
- Explicit requests for detail override the adaptive reply-length default. Button clicks/samples/model replies do not affect that signal.
- Keep loading feedback, cancellation, visible errors and reduced-motion support.
- Phase 3 work samples remain pasted text only. PDF/DOCX/XLSX input is approved as future scope, not yet implemented.
- Side-by-side work comparison is a separate deferred feature. It is NOT Interview Phase 3.
- Accounts, persistent storage, voice and avatars remain future work unless the actual repo demonstrates subsequent implementation.
- Avatar must support appearance description as an alternative to camera capture; camera is optional and not used for authentication.
